# Meta feed with AI scoring

The deal is to reduce how often I check my RSS feed and scroll through Hacker News pages to find only actually interesting articles and focus solely on them.

For this reason, I may look through all interesting RSS feeds and score the content based on my criteria to list only those texts, which are relevant, instead of scanning manually through titles and summaries.

By extension, to make it more usable, such feeds should already contain article content, or, if scored a low grade, simply a summary. This would provide the best experience, as all would be available directly from feed reader.

This should not be like feedly, or general AI newsfeeds, because it focuses on sources you pick expressively, and only provides a way to decide, which content is actually relevant and worth examining, without the hassle of sieving through them manually -- this may be useful for variety of content, like at Hacker News.
